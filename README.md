# CoolRoute ☀️

### Heatwave Safety Intelligence System

**CoolRoute helps people make safer decisions during extreme heat by turning heat-risk information into practical, location-aware guidance.**

Developed for the **MUET Khairpur Hackathon — CL-02 Challenge** by Team Twin Thread.

## 🌍 The Problem

Extreme heat can put people's health and daily activities at risk. Finding safer times to travel and locating nearby cooling or water resources can be difficult when useful information is scattered across different sources.

## 💡 Our Solution

CoolRoute is designed to make heat-safety information easier to understand and act on. It brings heat-risk insights and practical guidance together in one accessible interface.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Heat Risk Insights** — Understand heat conditions and associated risk.
- **Safer Travel Windows** — Identify more suitable times for outdoor travel based on available conditions.
- **Resource Map** — Explore available cooling and water resources.
- **Interactive Dashboard** — Access key information in one place.
- **Accessible Experience** — A user-friendly interface designed to make safety information easier to use.
